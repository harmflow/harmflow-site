Installing HarmFlow

HarmFlow is coming to the Mac App Store.

Until then, the community edition builds from source. The build instructions are in the Quick start of harmflow-app's README, which says what your Mac needs and which commands to run.

HarmFlow runs its tasks through Claude Code or Codex, signed in on your Mac. It never installs or updates them: have at least one of them in place before your first task.

  • First use: the five steps the app walks you through the first time you open it, up to the live executions screen with a first task.
  • The interface's languages: the three languages the app comes in, where to choose one in Settings, and why no other can be added.
  • Dates, times, durations, and sizes: how the app writes them in the interface's language, with the same execution in each of the three.
  • Codex's managed requirements: what the warnings mean when an organization's requirements do not allow the settings HarmFlow runs Codex with, and why tasks still run.
  • The Settings screen: what each of its sections holds, where the menu bar and autostart options are, and what it shows of the AI tools, the folders, and the access token.
  • Starting the daemon by hand: where Start daemon is when the daemon does not start with the system, what the dashboard shows while and after it starts, and why running now and starting with the system are separate.
