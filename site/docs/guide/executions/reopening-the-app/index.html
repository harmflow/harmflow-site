Closing and reopening the app

You can close the app while your tasks run and open it again later. Closing it stops no execution, and when the app opens again it shows what ran while it was closed. This page says what happens to an execution meanwhile, and what you find on opening the app.

While the app is closed

Closing the app and opening it again interrupts no execution. Executions are run by HarmFlow's daemon, which keeps running in the background whether you close the window, quit the interface from the menu bar, or press ⌘Q. An execution that is running goes on to its end, and a task whose schedule comes due starts its execution as usual. Closing or quitting the app is not stopping the daemon: Stop daemon… in the menu bar stops it, and asks first. While executions run, it lets you wait for them to finish, and the daemon stops then, or cancel them now, and they are recorded as interrupted. The menu bar options are in The Settings screen.

What the Executions screen shows

When the app opens, the Executions screen lists the executions that are running at that moment, one card each, including the ones that started while the app was closed. The executions that ended while it was closed appear in the Recent history panel beside the cards, each with the state it ended in, such as Completed.

An execution's whole history

Opening any of these executions shows its whole history of events, from its creation to its end, in order, each event once. The events it emitted while the app was closed are all there: an execution that is still running shows them, followed by the new ones as they arrive, and one that has ended shows the same history it would have shown had you followed it from the start.
